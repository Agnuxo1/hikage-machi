## question

Hikage-machi asks how open city data can support research on bus-stop shade. This update reports a data-intake milestone for Takamatsu, Japan. It is an application in development, not a finished solar-exposure tool.

## data

The actual city archive contains two hundred and sixty-eight thousand, one hundred and fifty-four building objects with unique identifiers. Two thousand, one hundred and sixty contain level-two detail elements. A separate Kotoden Bus timetable snapshot contains six hundred and fifty-eight stop records.

## checks

Ten numerical and data-consistency checks passed. Two different counting methods agreed across four hundred and eight building files. A separate reviewer checked source hashes, timetable relationships, and a sample of geometry. These checks establish consistency within their stated scope.

## limits

They do not establish building accuracy, sunlight exposure, cooling effects, or energy savings. The next research stage needs a reference shading calculation, dated service patterns, and validation on actual bus stops. Code, sources, and limitations are documented.
